Frequently asked questions

How do I play?

Place exactly one monkey in every coloured grove, one in every row and one in every column. Monkeys may never touch each other, not even diagonally. Tap once to mark a spot with an X (tap again to clear it), double-tap to perch a monkey, and drag to mark many spots at once. You can replay the guided tutorial from Settings.

I'm stuck. What can I do?

Use a helper: Bright Idea shows the next logical step and explains why, Parrot Marker crosses out impossible spots, and Monkey Drop places one monkey correctly. You earn helpers from the daily reward and Streak Chests.

Is there a guess-free solution to every puzzle?

Yes. Every puzzle has exactly one solution that can be reached with logic alone.

Will I lose my progress if I delete the app?

Yes. Progress is stored only on your device, so deleting the app also deletes your levels, streaks and helpers.

How do I change my ad privacy choices?

On iPhone, go to Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking. If you're in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, open Settings in the game and tap "Privacy & ad choices".
