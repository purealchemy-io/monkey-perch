Monkey Perch

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

Monkey Perch ("the App") is a puzzle game published by Pure Alchemy ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information is handled when you use the App.

Information we collect

We do not ask for, collect or store any personal information such as your name, email address or phone number. The App has no accounts.

Your game progress (levels solved, stars, streaks, helper counts, chosen avatar and settings) is stored only on your device. It is not sent to us. Deleting the App deletes this data.

Advertising

The App shows ads served by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process:

  • device identifiers, such as the advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, Advertising ID on Android) when you allow it, or an app-scoped identifier;
  • IP address and the approximate location derived from it;
  • information about your device, such as model, operating system and language;
  • information about how you interact with ads, and app usage and crash data.

On iOS, the App asks for your permission before using the advertising identifier for tracking. You can change this at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking. On Android, you can reset or delete your Advertising ID in your device settings. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the App asks for your consent to personalised ads, and you can change your choice later from the game's Settings.

Learn how Google uses data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
Google privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy

Rewarded videos

Watching a rewarded video is always optional. It gives you an in-game reward, such as an extra banana or helper.

Children

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

We may update this policy. Changes are posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
